🏁 **ACI LMGT3 Challenge 2026 — who's attempting the pre-quali?**

Some of us are considering entering the **ACI LMGT3 Challenge 2026**, the official championship run by ACI Sport on Le Mans Ultimate: single LMGT3 class, 35-car field, 6 races from Imola to Bahrain.

Registration closes **September 15**. If entries exceed the 35 available spots, pre-qualifying sessions on **September 17 and 20** (21:00–23:00 CET) decide who makes the official field.

We're building a kind of **Story Book** on the site for VSD's ACI adventure — not just tables and standings, but the story of this challenge chapter by chapter, from pre-quali to the end-of-season recap. To tell it properly from the start, not only once (and if) someone makes the official grid, we need to know now who's giving it a go.
